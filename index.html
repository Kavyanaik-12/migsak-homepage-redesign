What should a business website include?

A useful business website explains its services, shows credible information, works on mobile devices and gives visitors a clear way to make contact.

Website or mobile app?

A website is accessible through a browser. A mobile app may be more suitable when the business needs device capabilities or frequent user interactions. Choose based on actual user needs.

Which tasks can be automated?

Repetitive data entry, notifications and routine reporting can sometimes be automated. First map the existing workflow and check whether automation is appropriate.
